Publish your TikTok videos on your schedule

Emli is a publishing tool for TikTok creators. Each creator signs in with their own TikTok account and authorizes Emli through TikTok’s official Login Kit. Emli then uploads the videos and photos that creator made, to that creator’s own account, at the times the creator schedules — through TikTok’s official Content Posting API.

Emli never asks for or stores your TikTok password. The connection runs entirely through TikTok’s own authorization screen, and you can revoke it at any time in your TikTok settings.

Step 1 — Connect your TikTok account

Continue to TikTok and approve the connection. TikTok will show you exactly which permissions Emli requests before you decide: basic profile information (user.info.basic), publishing videos to your account (video.publish), and uploading videos as drafts (video.upload).

After you approve, TikTok sends you back to this page with a one-time authorization code. Step 2 below explains how that code finishes the connection in the Emli scheduler on your computer.

Prepare a post

Posting as: your connected TikTok account. Before anything is published, the Emli scheduler reads your account’s current creator info from TikTok and shows the exact account nickname it will post to, so you can confirm it is the right account.

All interactions are off by default. Turn on only the ones you want for this post.

By posting, you agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation

This page only prepares your choices. Posting itself runs through the Emli scheduler on your computer: the scheduler performs the upload with these exact settings and shows the live publishing status there. A published post may take a few minutes to appear on TikTok.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026 · Effective: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Emli application (“Emli”, “we”, “us”) receives and uses when you connect your TikTok account, and the choices you have about that information. Emli exists for one narrow purpose — publishing your own content to your own TikTok account on your schedule — and its data practices are deliberately limited to that purpose.

In short: Emli uses your TikTok authorization only to upload the videos and photos you create and schedule. Emli does not sell your information, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone other than TikTok, as needed to publish your content.

1.Information we receive

From TikTok, when you authorize Emli:

  • Your TikTok user identifier (open ID) and basic public profile information returned by TikTok’s Login Kit, such as your display name and avatar, used only to identify the connected account;
  • An access token and refresh token for the permissions you approved — including the video.publish scope — which Emli uses to upload content on your behalf. Emli never receives or stores your TikTok password.

Content and settings you provide:

  • The videos, photos, and captions you select for scheduled publishing;
  • The dates, times, and related settings in the publishing schedule you create.

Technical records:

  • Basic operational records of upload activity — for example, whether a scheduled upload succeeded or failed and the resulting post identifier — kept so the Service can confirm your schedule was carried out and diagnose errors.

Emli does not collect information about your followers, your messages, other users, or your activity on TikTok beyond what is listed above.

2.How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • authenticate the connected TikTok account and keep the authorization active;
  • upload your content to your TikTok account at the times you scheduled, through TikTok’s official Content Posting API;
  • show you the status of your scheduled and completed uploads;
  • maintain the security and reliability of the Service and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the Service for you.

3.How information is shared

Your content and authorization tokens are transmitted to TikTok solely so that TikTok can receive and publish your scheduled uploads; TikTok’s handling of that content is governed by TikTok’s own Privacy Policy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not disclose it to other third parties except where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of the Service and its users.

4.Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored in access-controlled storage and are transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. Access to stored information is limited to what is necessary to operate the Service. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we apply safeguards proportionate to the limited data Emli handles.

5.Retention

We keep authorization tokens and your publishing schedule only while your TikTok authorization remains active and you continue to use the Service. Content files are kept only as long as needed to complete their scheduled upload. If you revoke Emli’s access in your TikTok settings or stop using the Service, the associated tokens and schedule data are deleted, and any remaining operational records are removed within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

6.Your choices and rights
