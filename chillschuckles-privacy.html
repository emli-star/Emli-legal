Privacy Policy — ChillsChuckles

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Effective: November 8, 2026

ChillsChuckles is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channels “The Haunted Lady” (@chillsandchuckles-n3m) and “War Untold stories”. It is used by the owner only, to upload and manage videos on his own YouTube channels through the official YouTube Data API. This policy explains what information the application receives and how it is used.

In short: ChillsChuckles uses the owner’s Google authorization only to upload and manage the owner’s own videos on his own channels. It does not sell information, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone other than Google/YouTube, as needed to perform those uploads.

1. Information we receive

  • Google authorization tokens (access token and refresh token) for the YouTube permissions the owner approved, including uploading videos and managing channel content. The application never receives or stores the owner’s Google password.
  • Channel information for the owner’s own channels (channel identifiers, titles, and basic statistics) returned by the YouTube Data API.
  • Content the owner provides: video files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and publishing schedules created by the owner.
  • Operational records of upload activity (for example, whether an upload succeeded and the resulting video identifier), kept to confirm the schedule was carried out and to diagnose errors.

2. How we use information

Information is used only to authenticate the owner’s channels, upload and manage the owner’s videos on YouTube, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, check upload and channel status, and maintain the security and reliability of the tool. It is not used for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

3. How information is shared

Content and tokens are transmitted to Google/YouTube solely so YouTube can receive and publish the owner’s uploads, through the YouTube API Services. By using this application, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not sell, rent, or trade information, and we do not disclose it to other third parties except where required by law.

4. Storage and security

Authorization tokens are kept in access-controlled storage on the operator’s own server and are transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. Access is limited to operating the tool.

5. Retention and revocation

Tokens are kept only while the owner uses the tool. The owner can revoke the application’s access at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party access), which immediately stops all future uploads. On revocation or request, stored tokens and schedules are deleted.

6. Children’s privacy

This tool is operated by an adult for his own channels and is not directed to anyone under 18.

7. Changes to this policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
